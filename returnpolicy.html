Return Policy
Hassle-free returns within 15 days
We offer FREE returns on all eligible orders. No questions asked.

Return Window

You can initiate a return within 15 days of delivery. After 15 days, we are unable to accept returns or exchanges.

Eligible Items

  • Items must be unworn and unwashed
  • Original tags must still be attached
  • Item must be in original packaging
  • No stains, damage, or alterations

Non-Returnable Items

  • Innerwear (briefs, boxers, bras, panties)
  • Swimwear
  • Earrings and pierced jewellery (hygiene)
  • Items purchased during flash sales (unless defective)

Refund Process

Once we receive and inspect your return, a refund will be processed to your original payment method within 5–7 business days. For COD orders, refunds are credited to your bank account.

  • Refund processed after quality check
