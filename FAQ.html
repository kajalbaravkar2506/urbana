Frequently Asked Questions
Find quick answers to common questions
Orders & Delivery
How long does delivery take?
Standard delivery takes 3–5 business days. Express delivery (1–2 days) is available at checkout for select pin codes.
Is free delivery available?
Yes! Orders above ₹999 qualify for free standard delivery. For orders below ₹999, a flat shipping fee of ₹49 applies.
Can I track my order?
Yes, once your order is shipped you'll receive an SMS and email with a tracking link. You can also track your order from your account page.
Returns & Exchanges
What is the return policy?
We offer a 15-day easy return policy. Items must be unworn, unwashed, and have original tags attached. Innerwear and accessories are non-returnable for hygiene reasons.
How do I exchange a size?
Visit the Contact Us page or email [email protected] with your order ID and the size you need. We'll arrange a pickup and send the new size within 5–7 days.
Payments
What payment methods are accepted?
We accept UPI, Credit/Debit Cards, Net Banking, Wallets (Paytm, PhonePe), and Cash on Delivery for orders up to ₹5000.
Is Cash on Delivery available?
Yes, COD is available for orders up to ₹5000 and for select pin codes. A ₹30 COD handling fee applies.
Products & Sizing
How do I find the right size?
Each product page has a size guide. We recommend measuring your chest, waist, and hips and comparing with our size chart. When in doubt, size up.
Are products genuine?
100%. All products sold on Urbana are sourced directly from our manufacturing partners. We do not sell replicas or second-quality items.
