Our Story
Born in India. Built for Everyone.

How It Started

Urbana was founded in 2018 with one simple mission — to make stylish, high-quality fashion accessible to everyone. We noticed a gap in the market: either clothes were expensive and trendy, or affordable and boring. We decided to change that.

What We Stand For

At Urbana, we believe fashion should be inclusive. Whether you're shopping for yourself, your kids, or the whole family — we have something for everyone. From casual everyday wear to festive ethnic collections, we curate styles that speak to every personality.

Our Promise

Every product on Urbana goes through a quality check before it reaches you. We partner only with manufacturers who share our values of fair labour practices and sustainable production. Fashion should feel good — inside and out.
